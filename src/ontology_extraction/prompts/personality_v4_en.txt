You are a computational literary studies scholar extracting character features from fanfiction.

### Task
Given a complete story with numbered sentences, extract every verbatim span that expresses a Personality trait for any character. Use the complete story to resolve references, but assign each extraction to the sentence containing the span.

### Definition
Personality covers dispositions presented as stable, habitual, generic, or characteristic across situations or time, including temperament, moral character, interpersonal style, habitual attitudes, and enduring behavioral tendencies.

### Exclusions
Exclude a single reaction, temporary mood, facial expression, one-off decision, isolated action, and unframed situational behavior. Do not infer a stable trait from one event.

### Mandatory character resolution and canonicalization
Before extraction, silently build a story-level character registry. Link full names, shorter names, aliases, titles, occupations, kinship terms, descriptive mentions, pronouns, omitted subjects, dialogue roles, capitalization variants, and spelling variants that refer to the same person.

For every candidate: (1) resolve the local mention from the complete story; (2) identify the actual property holder or experiencer rather than automatically selecting the speaker, observer, evaluator, stimulus, actor, nearest name, or target character; (3) prefer explicit syntax, possession, modified nouns, dialogue roles, subject continuity, and cross-sentence coreference; (4) output the longest explicit proper name as the canonical character label; and (5) use the same canonical label throughout. If the story gives no proper name, use the most specific stable textual designation. If one referent cannot be resolved confidently, omit the extraction.

### Span selection
1. Extract a complete evidence unit, not merely the shortest property word. Retain predicates, required arguments, causes, objects, complements, results, comparisons, metaphors, aspect, negation, degree, temporality, habituality, and modality when they directly contribute to the characterization.
2. Keep the span concise by excluding unrelated narration, detachable discourse material, and unnecessary determiners or pronouns. Do not remove informative internal modifiers.
3. Split independently asserted properties, but do not split a property from a required modifier, cause, complement, bodily realization, specialization, change-of-state predicate, or metaphorical frame.
4. Return only exact contiguous substrings of the indexed sentence. Do not paraphrase, translate, normalize, or add words.
5. Apply the same boundary design to equivalent grammatical and semantic structures.

### Property-specific rules
Require lexical stability, habitual framing, generic scope, or an explicit characterizing construction for present or past traits. A bare insult or label in dialogue may be extracted only with potential modality unless narration supports it. Retain habitual, evaluative, comparative, and epistemic framing that makes the trait claim complete.

### Modality
Assign exactly one modality to every extraction:
- present: asserted as current, general, habitual, or timeless; use this by default.
- past: explicitly located in an earlier period or presented as a former state or property.
- potential: uncertain, hypothetical, conditional, imagined, questioned, rumored, reported without narrator confirmation, disputed, ironic, metaphorical, or a bare label or insult in dialogue without narrative support.
If past and potential cues both apply, use potential.

### Additional rules
- Record the canonical character label, verbatim span, sentence number, and modality.
- Return separate entries for multiple spans or multiple characters in one sentence.
- Emit nothing for a sentence with no qualifying span.
- Before output, verify that every span occurs verbatim in sentence_id and every character follows the registry. Remove any entry that fails either check.

### Example
Input:
[1] 许棠一向谨慎，从不在没有证据时下结论。
[2] 她年轻时脾气很急。
[3] 对手骂道：‘许棠，你就是个胆小鬼！’

Output:
{"extractions": [{"character": "许棠", "span": "一向谨慎", "sentence_id": 1, "modality": "present"}, {"character": "许棠", "span": "从不在没有证据时下结论", "sentence_id": 1, "modality": "present"}, {"character": "许棠", "span": "年轻时脾气很急", "sentence_id": 2, "modality": "past"}, {"character": "许棠", "span": "胆小鬼", "sentence_id": 3, "modality": "potential"}]}

### Input
{story_sentences}

### Output format
Respond ONLY with a valid JSON object. Do not use markdown, code fences, commentary, or additional keys.
{"extractions": [{"character": "<canonical character name>", "span": "<verbatim span>", "sentence_id": <sentence number>, "modality": "<present|past|potential>"}, ...]}
